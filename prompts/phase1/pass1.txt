- SaaS or software selling (including software subscriptions)
- retention, renewal, upsell, cross-sell, refund-reduction, churn-reduction responsibility
- pipeline or funnel responsibilities

- Territory is where the candidate SOLD or covered customers. Do NOT extract candidate location as territory (e.g. "Based in India", "Lives in Bangalore", "Located in Chennai", "Remote from India").
- Do NOT assign canonical tags
- Skip evidence already captured in role_analysis
- Skip irrelevant text (education facts, personal info, hobbies)
